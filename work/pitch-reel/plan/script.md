## Talking points, if you'd rather speak freely
- 11 founders, half a day, 10 min pitch + 5 min my questions → keep building or not
- "These are some things I think you should keep in mind when you're pitching"
- Founder story: how you discovered the problem, why it matters to you (founder–market fit)
- Competitive landscape: incumbents, their offering, your differentiation; a mention is enough
- User journey: at every step, what's your plan? I need to know you've thought of it
- Vision + ICP: product/service vision, where it's going; 2–3 segments is fine
- A lot changes once there's proof of work, but for an initial pitch, keep these in mind
