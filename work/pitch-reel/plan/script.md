## Talking points, if you'd rather speak freely
- 11 founders, one day, 10 min pitch + 5 min my questions → keep building or not
- Your story: how you found the problem, why it matters to *you*
- What's already out there: competitors, a mention is enough
- User journey: comes from talking to users, primary research
- Vision + ICP: 2–3 groups is fine, research tells you who
- Save this
